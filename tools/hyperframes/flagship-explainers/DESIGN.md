# POLAR Flagship Explainers — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A cinematic black-metal enterprise interface where cayenne-orange energy, electric cyan intelligence, and approved POLAR character imagery operate inside a disciplined technical grid. Every frame should feel like an official BI POLARIZE system activation rather than a generic product commercial.

## Colors

- Void Black `#020405` — primary canvas
- Charcoal `#0B1114` — structural depth
- Cayenne Orange `#FF4D00` — action, borders, calls to action
- Electric Cyan `#00E5FF` — intelligence glow and secondary energy
- Polar White `#F7FBFC` — primary typography

## Typography

- Primary display: bold geometric sans-serif, rendered with a stable system sans fallback in CI
- Technical labels and captions: monospace

## Motion

- Strong directional entrances with controlled deceleration
- POLAR may float subtly after landing
- No infinite repeats
- Captions transition in discrete readable beats
- Final frame resolves cleanly into the division call to action

## Layout

- 1920 × 1080 landscape
- Copy left, approved division logo and POLAR stance right
- Captions remain inside a dedicated lower-third shell
- Approved imagery must use `object-fit: contain`

## What NOT to Do

- No stock footage or stock characters
- No unapproved logo recreations
- No neon rainbow palette
- No generic corporate-blue presentation styling
- No excessive glitching that harms readability
- No HeyGen, substitute avatars, or legacy POLAR artwork
