# BPEI Full Blueprint Video Design

## Style Prompt
A cinematic classified-technology film for BI POLARIZE ENTERPRISES, INC. Black and gunmetal foundations split by controlled fire and ice energy. P.O.L.A.R. appears as the trusted operational guide, while text, diagrams, and approved brand imagery move with deliberate precision. The visual language should feel engineered, premium, unconventional, and institutional rather than flashy for its own sake.

## Colors
- Void Black: #020409
- Gunmetal: #101820
- Electric Cyan: #00E5FF
- Volcanic Orange: #FF5500
- Molten Gold: #FFB300
- Signal White: #EFF8FF

## Typography
- Headings: Arial Black, Montserrat, sans-serif
- Body and system labels: Arial, Inter, sans-serif
- Technical readouts: Courier New, monospace

## Motion
- Smooth camera pushes and parallax
- Fire and ice energy sweeps between scenes
- Staggered text entrances with varied easing
- Deliberate scan lines, blueprint grids, and controlled glow
- No random glitching

## What NOT to Do
- No generic corporate blue gradients
- No rapid unreadable cuts
- No forced audio tricks or comedy effects
- No rejected or unapproved images
- No excessive glitch spam
- No fake loading screens
