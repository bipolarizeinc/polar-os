# POLAR Website Microvideos — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Create concise cinematic system-interface microvideos for BI POLARIZE ENTERPRISES, INC. using the approved POLAR character and corporate polarity language. The canvas is dark, high-contrast, technical, and premium, with controlled fire-and-ice accents. Motion should feel intelligent, responsive, and deliberate rather than loud or chaotic.

## Colors
- Obsidian Black `#020409` — primary canvas
- Electric Cyan `#00E5FF` — system intelligence, active states
- Volcanic Orange `#FF5500` — energy, urgency, polarity counterpoint
- Molten Gold `#FFB300` — verification, premium accents
- Frost White `#EFF8FF` — primary readable text

## Typography
- Montserrat — primary display and interface headings
- Inter — body and support copy
- Courier New — terminal and system-status labels

## Motion
- Short 0.35–0.8 second entrances
- Precise directional reveals and polarity sweeps
- Controlled scale, glow, and parallax only
- Use deterministic GSAP timelines
- End every clip in a clean, stable hero frame

## What NOT to Do
- No stock corporate imagery
- No generic blue SaaS styling
- No cartoon bounce or comedy motion
- No random particles or infinite loops
- No unapproved voice, stock TTS, pitch shifting, or fallback narration
- No HeyGen assets or dependencies
